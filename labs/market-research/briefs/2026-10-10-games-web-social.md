## AI feasibility
Web games are the easiest asset for agents to build and test headlessly (canvas/Phaser/PixiJS under Playwright; deterministic sim tests). Portal limits: CrazyGames 50 MB initial / 250 MB total / 1,500 files [verified]; Poki limits not reached. Godot web export is heavy for these caps [assumed]. The binding constraint is not building but (a) originality/fun, which Poki and CrazyGames explicitly screen for, and (b) portal acceptance with no relationships and no outreach.

## Verdict: **WEAKENS** (for web portals as a reliable earner at our scale); web remains the cheapest place to learn
- Expected value for a no-audience AI operator: low. Unit economics are roughly $1-3 per 1,000 plays [assumed from a stale datapoint], so $100/month needs ~30-100k plays/month, and portals gate that traffic by curation. Treat expected 30-day revenue as $0-20 [assumed].
- Plausible shapes (1-3): (1) Short-session casual/puzzle or arcade game with rewarded-ad hooks, submitted non-exclusively to CrazyGames (no exclusivity, free to submit) plus itch.io; (2) daily-challenge puzzle on our own domain with share-card loop, where Poki-style 100% direct economics apply but traffic is entirely ours; (3) a Reddit Devvit game, only if the post-July-2026 funds model turns out to exist and pay; unverified, treat as a free option.
- Traps: Telegram tap-to-earn (crypto/dark-pattern, collapsed), Poki 5-year exclusivity before we have any proof, SEO page farms (scaled-content risk), anything needing our own ad stack before traffic exists.
- 30-day test cost: about $0-25 [assumed]: one game, built and headless-tested by agents, owner submits to CrazyGames (owner-only action) and posts on itch.io. Domain optional. Decision metric: CrazyGames Basic Launch progression (average playtime, retention vs benchmark) and reaching Full Launch/500 plays; plus the stage-gates thresholds (200 players, 25% D1 return, 4 min median). Kill if not through Basic Launch in 30 days or rejected as unoriginal twice.

## What would change my mind
- A verified (dashboard screenshot or statement) earnings number from a new-to-portal, unknown solo dev in 2025-26 above ~$300/month.
- Reddit's official successor to Developer Funds with published thresholds that a small game can reach.
- Our first game clearing CrazyGames Full Launch quickly (would raise portal acceptance odds).
- Evidence that Poki/CrazyGames accept predominantly AI-built games at normal rates.
