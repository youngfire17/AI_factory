## 4. Compounding from $500 (ASSUMED: all profit reinvested, 1 capital turn/month, month-1 ramp ignored, eBay new-seller cap of ~$500/mo [claimed] would slow months 1-3)
Time cap [assumed]: 10 h/week, 0.5 h per item all-in (source, photo, list, message, ship) => ~85 items/mo x ~$8 net = ~$650/mo profit ceiling at ~$25 average cost items.

Reading: capital is not the constraint after ~$2,500 working capital; time is. Doubling profit needs higher-ticket items (lower hours per $ profit) not more units. Hours per $1,000 profit at the assumed rates: ~60-70 h [assumed]. Income at 10% scenario never exceeds ~$150/mo in year one; neither scenario is "life changing"; the base case needs the 20% monthly net to be real, which I could not verify.

## 7. Verdict
**Verdict: supports as a first rung only (unclear as a compounding engine).** It is the fastest way to generate first real dollars and to learn sourcing/demand, but the evidence for margins is vendor [claimed] and time, not capital, caps it near a few hundred dollars/month at 1-2 h/day. Good rung to build cash and pattern recognition, poor place to park growing capital.

Top 3 plays for $500-$2,000 (all need measurement):
1. **Thrifted/garage-sale retro games and small lots** (examples: loose DS/GBA/N64 titles bought $3-10; claimed sale values $30-90, CIB rarities >$300). Net $200-500/mo [assumed], 6-10 h/wk, 5 h/wk of that is physical sourcing. Test: buy 20 games/lots for <=$100 total; list all on eBay with sold-comp checks; measure net per item and days-to-sell after 30 days.
2. **Retail clearance LEGO/toys to eBay (sealed)** with BrickEconomy/Brickset tracking. LEGO sell-through 57%, avg $28.50 [claimed]. Net $100-300/mo [assumed], 5-8 h/wk. Test: buy 5 clearance sets (~$150), compare eBay sold comps beforehand; target >=25% net after fees and shipping.
3. **Amazon online arbitrage (FBM first to avoid FBA fees)** with SellerAmp ($19.95/mo). Net $100-400/mo [assumed], 6-8 h/wk. Test: free trial, 10 products at <=$300 total, ROI floor 30%, confirm ungated before buying.
Not recommended at $500: Pokemon sealed (supply-limited, correcting) and liquidation pallets (freight, shrink, slow).

## What would change my mind
- Toward stronger support: measured net >=25% blended with <=0.5 h/item across 30+ sold items, or a neutral dataset showing sustained margins.
- Toward weaker: net <10% after returns and shipping in the first 30 items, eBay holds/limits stalling cash, or time per item >1h.
- I could not verify Mercari/Poshmark/TCGplayer fees, eBay payout rules, Keepa pricing, or power-tool margins; a primary read of those could change channel choice.
