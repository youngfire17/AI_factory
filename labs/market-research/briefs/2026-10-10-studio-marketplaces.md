## 3. The factories: ideas, workflow, per-asset math
**Per-asset net income per month, [assumed] from the weak tiers above:** pessimistic $1, middle $4, good $10 (after fees). Basis: the unsourced Etsy tiers imply ~$5-15 per listing in surviving shops; the verified-by-Flippa-style plugin portfolio implies ~$11 per plugin after six years; itch.io anecdotes imply well under $5. Because most listings earn $0, the middle case already assumes you keep and multiply only the listings that sell.
| Target / month | Assets at $1 | at $4 | at $10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $100 | 100 | 25 | 10 |
| $500 | 500 | 125 | 50 |
| $1,000 | 1,000 | 250 | 100 |
Reading: $100/mo is plausible with 25-100 live assets and a few months of ranking; $500 needs 125+ assets unless a few "hits" carry the shop; $1,000 is a year-2 target. Reinvestment loop: spend profit on more listings in the niches whose first assets sold, not on ads.

**A. Etsy + Payhip niche workbooks.** Ideas: candle/soap pricing and wholesale workbook; cleaning-business quote and route tracker; dog-walker/groomer booking and profit sheet; wedding-vendor budget by trade; contractor estimate and change-order tracker; Verifactu/autonomo invoice tracker (Spanish; deadline-driven, per prior brief). Workflow: keyword check (20 searches; top-10 listings with dated reviews) -> Claude drafts the sheet with formulas, validation, sample data and a "how to use" tab -> run a formula/test pass (wrong math is the failure mode) -> mockups from real screenshots of the file -> listing copy with the Etsy AI disclosure -> owner uploads. Cross-list the same file on Payhip (5%) for Pinterest traffic.
**B. TpT.** Ideas: Google Slides escape rooms by standard; self-checking Sheets math fluency sets; seasonal science labs. Workflow: choose grade/standard -> Claude drafts, **a second pass checks every answer key and image** -> classroom-ready format. Risk: the Chalkbeat story shows reviewers and TpT punish error-laden AI output. Start only with answer-key-heavy, text-only products we can machine-verify (math, grammar).
**C. Game-dev assets.** Ideas: Unity editor utilities, 2D shader packs, procedural tileset/dungeon generators, UI kits built from vector code, sound packs generated from non-AI synths. Code and algorithmic output does not trip itch's disclosure rule; any gen-AI art must be disclosed. Workflow: Claude writes tool + docs + demo scene -> automated tests + Unity compile check -> screenshot GIFs -> itch.io first (free to list), Unity/Fab second.
**D. Micro plugins/extensions.** Ideas: Woo order-note/CSV exporters, bulk downloader (per prior brief), a niche formatter. Each needs a distinct purpose to avoid the repetitive-content rule; GPL compliance check for WP.
**E. Vector/icon packs.** Ideas: 200-icon consistent sets for niche trades, labeled as generative AI on Adobe. Test only after A-D because per-asset pay is cents and review rejections are likely; consistency and no trademarks are the bar.

## 4. QA and originality standard (keeps us off the "AI slop" list)
1. Every asset solves one named job for one named buyer; no generic "ultimate bundle" filler.
2. Verifiable output only: formulas tested, answer keys machine-checked, code compiled and unit tested.
3. Real screenshots or recordings of the real file; no fake mockups.
4. Disclose AI where the platform asks (Etsy listing text, itch AI Disclosure, Unity listing, Adobe checkbox, KDP if generated).
5. No copied competitor text or layouts; no brand, character or trademark references; no medical, legal or compliance guarantees.
6. Hard cap on release pace: at most 3-5 new listings per platform per week, so volume does not itself look like spam [assumed].
7. Red-team check before each listing batch (per CLAUDE.md); owner uploads.

## 5. Obstacle -> solution
| Obstacle | Solution / pivot |
|---|---|
| Most listings earn $0 | Treat each as a cheap test with a kill rule (60 days, under 3 stranger sales); double down only on the niche that sells; budget for ~25 tests |
| No audience | Pick marketplaces with search intent (Etsy, TpT, Unity/itch search, CWS); long-tail keywords; one Pinterest pin per listing; no outreach |
| Etsy/TpT can demote AI-looking listings | Disclose, add real screenshots, niche-specific formulas; avoid image-only or prompt-output-only products |
| Canva/Shutterstock/Envato bar AI | Do not build for them; sell the same skills on Etsy, Payhip, Adobe Stock (labeled) |
| DriveThru bans mostly-AI text | Drop; pivot to code/tool assets (C) or sell TTRPG tools only if human-authored |
| Fees: Gumroad Discover 30%, Creative Market 40-50% | Use Payhip/own checkout; Etsy 6.5% + processing is cheaper |
| Per-asset income tiny | Choose assets with $10-$50 price points and re-use one build into several formats; bundle winners |
| Quality errors destroy trust (TpT) | Machine-verify keys; start with text-only math/grammar sets |
| Policies change (YouTube-style crackdowns) | Spread across 4-5 platforms; keep source files; keep a Payhip store of every asset |
| Primary policy pages blocked for me | Owner to read Etsy creativity page, TpT seller terms, Adobe AI FAQ and Canva terms once before G3 (5 minutes each) |
| Owner-only actions (accounts, listing, payout) | Prepare complete listing packets (files, copy, screenshots, disclosure text); owner uploads |

## 6. Verdict (supports / weakens / unclear)
**Unclear, leaning supports only for the "portfolio of cheap tests" form, and weakens the "any AI asset on any marketplace" form.** Evidence that unknown sellers earn is anecdotal or unsourced on every platform; policy evidence is good (AI is allowed with disclosure on Etsy, itch, Unity, Fab, Adobe, KDP-assisted, WordPress) and bars exist at Canva, Shutterstock, DriveThru (text) and probably Envato.
**Order:** (1) Factory A on Etsy+Payhip, (2) C on itch.io then Unity, running in parallel because it is free to list and Claude builds it well, (3) B on TpT (Basic plan first), (4) D one plugin or extension, (5) E only if A-D show the owner can upload fast.
**30-day fair test (about 40 assets, ~$10-15 cash):** 20-24 Etsy/Payhip workbooks across 3 niches (about $5 in Etsy listing fees at $0.20), 8 TpT resources, 4-5 itch/Unity packs or tools, 1-2 extensions/plugins (Chrome fee not checked, [assumed] ~$5). Adobe vectors deferred. At 1-2 h/day, roughly 1-2 assets per day is plausible only because reuse is high [assumed].
**Measure per listing at day 30 and 60:** impressions, views, favorites, stranger sales, conversion (gate: at least 1%, at least 3 stranger sales), refunds, net after fees, hours per asset, rejections or demotions, review text.

## 7. What would change my mind
- A dated Etsy, TpT or Payhip sales export from any AI-assisted shop under 12 months old showing real per-listing sales (upgrades A/B).
- Etsy's creativity page or TpT's terms showing AI-generated digital files are suppressed or banned (downgrades A/B).
- Unity or Fab publisher revenue data for a new publisher (upgrades C).
- Canva or Envato confirming AI-assisted human-edited work is accepted (opens large marketplaces).
- Zero stranger sales across 40 listings by day 60 (kills the generic version of the thesis).
