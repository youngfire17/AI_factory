# Distribution engines for digital assets with no audience
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-10. **Lane:** how a $500, 1-2 h/day, no-audience operator gets buyers. Research only; nothing started. Builds on `2026-10-10-landscape-research.md` and `2026-10-10-assets-synthesis.md`.
**Tags:** [verified] = primary page read 2026-10-10. [claimed] = secondary or vendor blog. [assumed] = my estimate. **Most channel performance numbers below are [claimed]; no channel has paid-demand proof for our specific products.** Etsy's own fee, policy and Offsite Ads pages returned 403, so Etsy figures rest on search summaries quoting Etsy's policy text.

## 1. Primary-source facts that shape the plan
- **Pinterest** (policy.pinterest.com/en/community-guidelines): rules apply to synthetic content; **no AI-labeling or disclosure requirement stated** on that page; automation "not explicitly approved by Pinterest" is prohibited (approved partner tools are listed); repetitive or deceptive content made to earn money is prohibited. [verified]
- **Pinterest "AI modified" label** exists on image Pins, applied by creator opt-in or Pinterest classifiers; creators can appeal; artists report false labels on non-AI work (TechCrunch Apr 2025, Gigazine Feb 2026). [claimed]
- **Tailwind** (official Pinterest partner, posts via API): $29.99/mo monthly or $17.99/mo annual for scheduling, 300 scheduled Pins per month; its support page now advises **about 1-5 fresh Pins/day** (old 15-25/day advice retired), no duplicate image/URL spam, each Pin to ~10 or fewer boards. [verified] on tailwindapp.com/pricing and its support article.
- **YouTube inauthentic content** (support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392): July 2025 rename of "repetitious content"; covers template-made, mass-produced, interchangeable videos and AI content with no original perspective; slideshows or scrolling text with little commentary are examples; **reviewers look at channel theme, top/newest videos and metadata**; recurring formats are fine if each video has its own focus. [verified]
- **Google scaled content abuse** (developers.google.com spam-policies): many pages made mainly to rank, "regardless of how it was made"; examples include mass AI pages with no added value, stitching other pages without original value, multiple sites to hide scale. [verified]
- **Pew (Jul 2025):** 900 US adults, 68,879 searches; users clicked a result in **8% of visits with an AI summary vs 15% without**; ~1% clicked a link inside the summary. Google disputes the method. [verified]
- **Hacker News Show HN rules** (news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html): must be runnable or tryable, non-trivial, built by you and you discuss it; **no landing pages, sign-up pages or newsletters; no vote solicitation**; ideally no email gate. [verified]
- **MailerLite free plan:** 250 subscribers, 2,500 emails/mo; paid "Comfort" from ~$12/mo. [verified] on mailerlite.com/pricing (older blogs still say 1,000; they are stale).
- **Etsy Offsite Ads:** 15% fee if shop sales under $10,000 over 365 days (opt-out allowed while under), 12% once over (no opt-out after); fee only when an ad click leads to a sale within a ~30-day window; reported $100 per-order cap. [claimed] (quoted policy text via search; Etsy page 403).
- **CAN-SPAM:** working unsubscribe honored within 10 business days, valid postal address in every email, no B2B exemption, penalty up to ~$53,088 per email. [claimed] (secondary summaries; ftc.gov not read). GDPR: needs consent for EU subscribers, not read here, [assumed] standard double opt-in practice is enough.

## 3. Obstacle -> solution
| Obstacle | Solution / pivot |
|---|---|
| Brand-new Etsy shop has no reviews or sales history | Launch with 15-30 listings in ONE tight niche (e.g. one party-game theme family); price the first 2-3 low, then raise after reviews; owner gets first reviews from real buyers only (no fake or purchased reviews). Use Pinterest to supply outside traffic that Etsy search cannot give a new shop. |
| Etsy requires AI disclosure; prompt-only output is suppressed [claimed] | Add the AI-assistance line and "designed by seller" attribute; add real human value (original game mechanics, writing, layout checked by owner). Never sell raw generator output. |
| Ads lose money on cheap products | Math in section 5: at $5 net ~$4.20, break-even needs ~8% conversion at $0.35 CPC. Do not buy ads for sub-$10 items. Pivot: use ads only on a $15+ bundle, or to grow the email list. |
| Offsite Ads 15% fee can't be avoided once over $10k | Under $10k, opt out of Offsite Ads and absorb nothing; after $10k, price the 12% in. Pull traffic through Pinterest/email so fewer sales are Offsite-attributed. |
| Pinterest: unapproved automation and duplicate-pin spam get accounts limited | Use native scheduler or Tailwind (approved partner); 1-3 fresh, visually distinct pins per listing per day max, never repost the same image/URL; keep one pin to few boards. |
| Pinterest "AI modified" label may reduce clicks | Test labeled vs unlabeled only if Pinterest forces it; lead with real product mockups/screenshots (genuine content) rather than AI scenery; appeal false labels. |
| YouTube/TikTok punish templated AI content | Show the real product being used (gameplay, print-and-play demo, with spoken or written commentary); make each video a different angle; skip slideshows. Accept this channel is low priority for 30 days. |
| Email list needs traffic, free tier is only 250 | 250 is enough to measure opt-in rate and first conversions. Upgrade (~$12/mo) only after 100+ real subscribers. Store consent, add unsubscribe + postal address. Etsy buyer data use for marketing: **not verified**; collect emails only via our own page. |
| Reddit/Facebook ban self-promotion | Be a participant first (2-4 weeks, heuristic [claimed]); give free value in comments; link only where rules allow (weekly promo thread); disclose affiliation; one post per community. Pivot: post the free printable as the value. |
| Show HN/Product Hunt need a tryable product and presence | Reserve for the browser game or tool (not printables). Make it playable with no signup; owner replies live; no vote asking. Expect a spike, not a business. |
| SEO tool pages risk "scaled content abuse" | Few pages (under 20), each a working interactive demo or with original data; no keyword-permutation pages; roll out in batches and check indexing. Treat as a 6-month play. |
| AI Overviews cut clicks | Prefer queries where the user needs the artifact (download, play, print) rather than an answer; weight Pinterest/Etsy over informational SEO. |
| Time budget 1-2 h/day | Batch: one weekly 3 h AI production session, daily 15-20 min owner posting/replies. Cap total at ~10 h/wk. |

## 4. Ranking (for $500, 1-2 h/day, no audience)
1. **Etsy native + Pinterest, as a pair** (best demand fit: buyers already search; Pinterest supplies outside traffic; $0-18/mo).
2. **Email capture via free printable** (owned asset; slow but compounding; prerequisite for anything beyond Etsy).
3. **Niche communities, value-first** ($0; unproven; ban risk if sloppy).
4. **Single launch spike for the game/tool** (Show HN / itch / Reddit game subs; one-time, cheap).
5. **Borrowed-audience micro-tests** ($25-50 classified; needs owner outreach decision).
6. **Interactive-tool SEO** (long timeline; AI Overview and spam-policy risk).
7. **Short video** (no credible evidence; policy-sensitive).
8. **Paid ads** (only as measurement for >= $15 products or list building).

## 5. Break-even and the $25-$100 paid test
Net per sale = price minus fees. Etsy ~ $0.20 listing + 6.5% + ~3% processing + $0.25 [assumed], so a $5 printable nets ~$4.20 and a $19 bundle ~$16.8. Break-even conversion = CPC / net.
- $5 item, $0.35 CPC (Etsy Ads midpoint): needs **~8%**; claimed Etsy average is 2% [claimed] -> expected loss (~$17 CPA vs $4.20 margin).
- $19 bundle, $0.60 CPC (Pinterest/Meta low-mid): needs **~3.6%**; plausible only if Pinterest conversion hits the upper claimed band (2-4%).
- Test design: $50 total. $25 Etsy Ads on the best 1-2 listings ($1-2/day for 2 weeks) and $25 Pinterest promoted pin to the same product. At $0.50 CPC that buys ~100 clicks (~50 each). Decision: scale only if >= 2 paid sales in 100 clicks (>= 2% and CPA < net margin x 2); otherwise stop. 100 clicks is statistically thin; it detects disaster, not a winner. [assumed]
- This fits the stage-gate G2 cap ($25 cumulative) only for one half; the $50 needs an explicit owner exception (CLAUDE.md spend rule).

## 6. 30-day stack (about 9-11 h/week total; owner does all posting and accounts)
| Week | Hours | Actions | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | Owner opens Etsy shop + Pinterest business account + ESP (owner-only). AI produces 15 listings in one niche + 20 pin images + 1 free printable + landing page. Verify with 20 Etsy searches (price, review dates). | Listings live; pins queued |
| 2 | 10 | Publish remaining listings (to 25-30); 1-3 pins/day; 2-3 value comments in 2 communities (no links); email welcome sequence on. | Pin impressions, outbound clicks, Etsy views/visits |
| 3 | 9 | Iterate: replace weakest 30% of listing titles/pins; first newsletter classified test ($25-50) only if owner OKs outreach; post free printable in permitted threads. | Visits to listing, favorites, opt-in rate |
| 4 | 9 | Decision week: run the $50 paid test only if organic conversion looks promising; one Show HN/itch post if a tryable game exists. | Sales count, conversion, CPA vs margin |
**Scale metrics (from stage-gates digital products row):** >= 3 paid sales from strangers, listing conversion >= 1%, refunds < 10%, positive net after fees. Also: Pinterest outbound click rate >= ~0.5% of impressions [assumed], email opt-in >= 5% of landing visits [claimed range low end].
**Kill metrics:** 0 sales and <100 listing visits after 30 days despite 25+ listings and daily pins -> demand/discoverability failed; kill or switch niche. Any account warning (spam, AI policy) -> stop that channel. Spend cap reached with no sale -> kill.

## 7. Verdict
**Supports (weakly)** the bet that distribution is solvable at near-$0 cost, via Etsy search + Pinterest + a small email list, for printable/party-game products. **Unclear** on whether it makes profit: no verified payment data for our exact products, no Pinterest-attributed revenue, no lead-magnet conversion data. **Weakens** games-as-free-browser-bets, short-video farms, and paid ads on sub-$10 items.

**What would change my mind:**
- Toward stronger support: our own Etsy stats showing outside-referral conversion >= 1-2%; 3 stranger sales in 30 days; Pinterest outbound clicks at scale from a new account.
- Toward weaker: 25+ listings and daily pins yielding <100 visits; Etsy delisting for AI policy; Pinterest limiting the account; discovery that 20 niche searches show only old reviews (no recent buyers).

**Next cheapest test ($0, no owner money):** 20 Etsy searches in the party-game/printable niche logging price, review count and the 3 newest review dates; plus Pinterest search for 10 target keywords noting top pin age and which link to Etsy. Then (owner, ~$0-18) open one shop, 15 listings, 14 days of pins. Verify Etsy fee/policy numbers and Pinterest-to-Etsy attribution in the live dashboard.
