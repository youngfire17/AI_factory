## 4. VERDICT: top 3 patterns and the first test for each
Common honesty note: none has a verified path for a zero-audience operator. Expected outcomes are [assumed] from the distributions above. Treat the portfolio as roughly 6-10 cheap experiments, each killed at 60 days without 3 stranger sales (matches `charter/stage-gates.md`).

1. **Niche professional workbook on Etsy plus Payhip/Gumroad (pattern A).** First product: **"Small-batch candle and soap maker costing, pricing and wholesale workbook (Google Sheets and Excel)"**, modeled on the candle-maker template shop with 4,703 sales. Avoid safety or labeling claims. Price $12-$19. Cost about $5-$25 (listings, a mockup tool). Hours 8-12 for the build, 2/week afterward. Distribution: Etsy search (long-tail keywords) plus a Pinterest pin and one free lite version. Expected: 60% chance of $0 in 60 days; typical success $50-$300 over 3 months. Cheapest test: 1 listing, 5 keyword variants, check that Etsy's eRank-style search shows existing sellers with reviews dated in the last 90 days before building.
2. **One-time-price utility Chrome extension (pattern C).** First product: **a better bulk file/image downloader** (the Flippa-verified #5 earns about $361/mo at $9.99 with a 2.7-star incumbent and 31 reviews). Cost about $5 Chrome developer fee [assumed from memory, check]; payments via ExtensionPay (fee not checked). Hours 10-15. Distribution: Chrome Web Store search only. Expected: 50-60% of such extensions earn $0; a good outcome is $100-$400/mo after 6-9 months. Cheapest test: build the free version, get 500 weekly users in 6 weeks by listing keywords alone, then add the $9.99 tier. Kill if the install rate is under 20/week at week 4.
3. **Deadline-driven readiness kit sold as a document, not a promise (pattern B).** First product: **"Verifactu readiness checklist and invoice-field tracker for Spanish autonomos" (Spanish, Sheets/PDF, $19-$39)**. Deadlines (sources disagree on the legal text, vendor blogs [claimed]): 1 Jan 2027 for companies, 1 Jul 2027 for self-employed; the demand sign is BeeL, a Spanish freelancer invoicing SaaS, reportedly at 166 subscriptions (search snippet; page 404, [claimed]). Cost $0-$25. Hours 10. Distribution: Spanish SEO plus Etsy ES/Gumroad. Expected: bimodal, $0 to a few hundred; deadline pressure peaks Q2 2027. Needs a compliance-officer pass: no "certified" or "guaranteed compliant" language. Cheapest test: a single landing page or Etsy listing; kill on under 50 visits and 0 sales in 30 days.

**Avoid:** MCP/skill/n8n/prompt-pack stores as first bets (no verified buyers); paid newsletters and directories; Notion widgets/embeds and any feature a host platform can ship; generic AI policy or content generators; AI art on Etsy; anything whose proof is a creator with a 100k audience (#11, #12); SaaS needing cold outreach; buying listed micro-SaaS under $10k (TrustMRR listings such as ToonThis show $652 revenue asking $9k).

## 5. What would change my mind
- A dated Etsy sales page or Stripe/Payhip export for any AI-built niche template shop under 12 months old earning over $500/mo would upgrade pattern A.
- A verified (TrustMRR/Stripe) MCP, skill or n8n product above $1k MRR with a solo founder and no audience would upgrade pattern E.
- Evidence that NDISCompliant's collapse was churn from ChatGPT substitution (downgrade B) versus a one-time deadline spike (acceptable for a document product).
- Etsy policy page (403 for me) showing AI-built digital files are suppressed.
