## 3. AI-built games on Steam

- Rule [verified]: the Content Survey requires disclosure of AI-generated content that ships to players (art, sound, narrative, localization). It does NOT cover AI tools used only to speed up development. Valve reviews pre-generated AI content like any other and requires it match marketing and not infringe. Live-generated AI needs guardrail descriptions and a payment path for ongoing costs. (partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey). Answers can be hard to change after approval. This is compatible with policies.md only if we disclose honestly.
- Volume [claimed]: ~9,400 of ~53,600 releases (Jul 2023-Jul 2026) are AI-flagged. Flagged launches rose to ~530/month, about a third of new games by mid-2026 (fragwyz.substack.com).
- Performance [claimed]: AI-flagged titles reach 100+ reviews at ~55% of the non-AI rate. AI is ~33% of launches but only ~10-27% of sales, and the top two AI games per quarter make 45-79% of AI revenue. A separate Game Oracle study (9,879 games, Jan-Oct 2025) found disclosed-AI games averaged 4 vs 7 first-month reviews, ~52.6% fewer reviews after controls, and ratings of 84.6% vs 88.3% (notebookcheck.net). Both use review-count proxies and cannot isolate AI as the cause.
- Review-bombing: I found no evidence that quantifies an AI-specific review-bomb effect. Next Fest being "flooded" with AI games is anecdotal coverage.
- AI-built games earning: I found no named AI-built game with verified earnings. Could not verify.

## 4. Genre placement (evidence thin)

- Idle/incremental: low price, fast-growing supply, slightly better-than-average reach, but 97.5% of sub-$5 idlers miss 1,000 reviews [claimed]. Saturating quickly.
- Roguelike deckbuilder / roguelite: historically strong medians, now crowded [claimed, dated]. Content-heavy and balance-sensitive, so hard for AI to make fun.
- Survivors-like: a few viral hits (Vampire Survivors, Megabonk) amid many clones. No median found. Could not verify.
- Tycoon/management: I found a ranged, unsourced figure (colony sim $150-400k for 100+ review games) [claimed, weak]. No reliable median.
- Puzzle / cozy: puzzle, arcade, platformer medians ~$1,000 or less [claimed]. Cozy not found separately.

## 5. Verdict

**Supports / weakens / unclear: WEAKENS** the bet that a Steam launch is a good use of the first $500 for a no-audience operator.

Expected value [assumed, my reasoning from the claimed base rates]: a typical no-audience premium launch is more likely to earn under $1,000 gross than to recoup the $100 fee. Steam's cut takes 30% and refunds take more. Our AI-disclosure drag [claimed] points the same way. The upside tail exists (Zero Stress King shows a $5 solo idle game can gross ~$160k), but I cannot estimate its probability from verified data and it has been getting less likely each year.

Plausible shapes, if any (all [assumed] judgement, not evidence):
1. Low-price ($2.99-$4.99) idle/incremental or idle-defense hybrid with real depth and a free demo. It suits AI production and has one recent comparable. Supply is saturating.
2. Small-scope roguelite/tower-defense with a demo, only if the sim and balance tests show it is genuinely fun.
3. Co-op or streamer-friendly shapes carry the biggest upside but need networking, playtesting and art quality that AI plus an owner cannot reliably deliver. Skip.

Minimum marketing [assumed]: a coming-soon page 3-6 months ahead, a demo with Next Fest participation, and 5k+ wishlists before launch. The only wishlist-to-revenue figure I found (<5k wishlists gives <$15k first month) is itself weak. Reaching 5k wishlists with no audience and no outreach (charter bars cold outreach) is the real blocker, and I have no verified evidence it is achievable at $0-$75. The $100 fee plus a possible Next Fest/page timeline also exceeds the G3 $75 spend cap and would need an explicit owner decision.

## What would change my mind
- A verified (VGI/Gamalytic/Alinea dashboard) 2025-26 median and top-quartile for the Idler or incremental tag showing top-quartile gross well above ~$5k.
- At least one verified AI-built, AI-disclosed game grossing >$10k from strangers.
- Evidence that a free browser/itch demo can reach several thousand wishlists organically for a no-audience developer.
- Our own browser prototype hitting the G3 to G4 thresholds (retention and return-visit), which de-risks the "is it fun" half.

## Next cheapest test ($0)
Ship or prepare the free browser/itch.io version first (already the charter's track). Record whether strangers return and whether any email or wishlist-style signal appears. In parallel, have the owner pull 20-30 comparable $2.99-$4.99 Idler/idle-defense Steam pages (read-only, no account needed) and record launch month, review count and price, to build a primary-data comp set. Defer the $100 fee until the free version clears G4 thresholds.
