# Synthesis: where should the first $500 (and the first hours) go?
Co-CEO read of the seven asset briefs (`2026-10-10-assets-*.md`). Retrieved 2026-10-10. Tags as in the briefs. **Research only; nothing here has been started.**
Owner constraints: $500 now, more capital can be added; 1–2 h/day; light human contact OK; no audience/skills/tools; AI does most of the work.

## 1. The honest headline
1. **No lane has verified, repeatable compounding from $500.** No documented case shows a reinvested, three-rung climb from under $2k with no audience and independent proof (ladders brief). Income figures everywhere are self-reported.
2. **At $500 the binding constraints are demand and attention, not capital.** Resale hits a time ceiling (~$650/mo at ~10 h/week [assumed]) before it hits a capital ceiling.
3. **Only labor-funded routes have positive expected value** in the capital model (12-month, probability-weighted, [assumed]): service-funded ≈ $1,617 ending capital (median ~$1,010); resale ≈ $925 (median $385; 60% chance below $500); buy-and-improve a small web asset ≈ $457. No scenario reaches a sustained $1,000/mo net inside a year.
4. **Buying existing assets only begins to work at ~$5–10k** (Flippa shows nothing credible under ~$2k; $5–10k stores reportedly earn $500–900/mo, seller-reported). Documented outcomes are mixed (one buyer: 2 of 4 sites lost money).
5. **A lot of "AI business" income content is fraud.** FTC-documented "AI storefront" schemes (e.g. Click Profit, fee ≥ $45k; Amazon terminated ~95% of its stores) [verified for Click Profit]. Treat any "turnkey AI business for sale" as hostile until proven.

## 7. Decisions needed from the owner
1. **Local outreach:** is pitching ~20 local business owners (email/phone/DM) acceptable? It is the likely way to land first service clients and vending sites; marketplaces are shrinking (Upwork active clients −4%, Fiverr buyers ≈ −22% [claimed]).
2. **Added capital:** how much and by when? Under ~$2k, buying assets is not credible; ~$5–10k opens R4.
3. **First cash engine:** services vs resale vs both at half speed.
4. **Spend rule:** acquisitions and any single spend over the old $25/$75 caps need an explicit exception.
