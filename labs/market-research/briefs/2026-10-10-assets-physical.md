## Verdict by asset
**Realistic at $500-$5k (with caveats):**
1. **Vending, 1-3 used snack/drink machines with card reader** (about $1.5-4k). Concrete first purchase: a refurbished combo or snack unit in the $1.6-2.5k range (the Hustle survey cites $1.6k and $2.5k purchases) plus a card reader, ONLY after a signed site. Expected net: $50-150 per machine per month in the weak case, $300-400 if the site is strong [mostly claimed]. Not "light contact": site acquisition is cold approaches. Gate-compliant cheapest test below.
2. **Bounce house or party-gear rental, 1 unit** (about $2-5k incl. insurance). Concrete first purchase: one commercial-grade 13x13 bounce house plus blower, only after quotes from 2-3 sellers and a GL quote. Net range: unknown; arithmetic from vendor figures (4 rentals/mo x $150 in season, minus insurance) suggests low hundreds/month averaged over a year [assumed]. Inbound demand means no cold approaches. Delivery needs a vehicle and storage; confirm local inspection/permit rules.
3. **Turo only if the owner already has a suitable car**; buying a car for this is NOT recommended at this budget (depreciation, claims risk). Net range $300-700/month is plausible but unproven [claimed]. Platform disclaims costs, and host horror stories involve five-figure out-of-pocket losses.

**Fantasy at this budget:** self-serve car wash, EV charging, laundromat, self-storage buildings, RV rental, claw-machine routes at scale, bulk gumball (economics of ~$1-2/machine/mo from the one route listing seen), ATM routes unless verified by the owner's own quotes.

**Honest note:** none of these produce idle-game-style compounding at $500. Realistic net from 1-3 units is perhaps $100-600/month combined [assumed], so doubling the asset base takes 6-18 months. Reinvestment loop: net cash buys the next unit in the same proven category (vending route, second inflatable).

## What would change my mind
- Real operator ledgers (Reddit r/vending, BiggerPockets, or the owner's own pilot) showing median net >$200/machine/month at used-machine prices -> upgrade vending.
- A local Turo/Getaround-like data pull (listing counts, booked days) for the owner's city showing >60% utilization on cheap cars -> upgrade Turo.
- Local data showing weekend inflatable rental demand unmet (competitor calendars, search volume) and a GL quote under ~$600/yr -> upgrade bounce house.
- A property manager or business agreeing to host a machine commission-free -> removes the largest vending risk.

## Next cheapest tests (all $0, no accounts, no contacting third parties by the agent)
1. Vending: prepare a target list of 30 nearby sites (offices 30+ employees, auto shops, gyms) and a pitch script; owner makes 20 pitches; success = 1 signed trial placement before any purchase. Cost: $0 and ~4 hours owner time.
2. Bounce house: AI collects competitor pricing and availability within 15 miles, a local permit check, and 3 insurance quote requests; decide go/no-go with data. Cost: $0.
3. P2P gear: owner lists one item he already owns (tool/camera) on a platform for 30 days; payment evidence = bookings. Cost: $0.
Owner-only actions: creating listings/accounts, signing site agreements, insurance purchase.
