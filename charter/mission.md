# Mission and operating constraints

**Goal:** build a portfolio of digital assets that generates real revenue, run mostly by AI staff, with the owner (Justin) providing oversight, account ownership, and final testing.

## Hard constraints (from the owner)
- Starting capital: **$500**. Reinvest proceeds; do not withdraw early profit.
- Minimal human labor. Owner's role: approve gates, own the accounts/payouts, final-test builds, make the call on anything irreversible.
- No employees, no inventory, no cold outreach to strangers.
- Prefer assets that compound: things we still own and can improve after launch.

## What "success" means (in order)
1. **Learn cheaply.** Each bet answers a question about demand for ~$0–$75.
2. **First dollar from a stranger.** Proof a product can earn from people who don't know us.
3. **A repeatable loop.** Revenue from a bet exceeds its marginal cost and can be reinvested.
4. **Portfolio.** Several bets earning, with proceeds funding the next.

## Honest framing
- Most small digital bets earn $0. The system's job is to find the few that don't, quickly and cheaply, and to stop spending on the rest.
- AI can build and test most of a game or product. It cannot guarantee a game is fun, cannot buy attention, and cannot create or own marketplace accounts. Those limits shape the plan (see `strategy.md`).
- Every number we use is tagged: **[verified]** (checked against a primary source, with link and date), **[claimed]** (from a source we haven't checked), **[assumed]** (our own estimate). Decisions may not rest on [claimed] numbers above the spend gates in `stage-gates.md`.
