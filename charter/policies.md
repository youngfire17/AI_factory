# Operating policies

## Integrity
- Never present [claimed] or [assumed] numbers as fact. Never fabricate sales, users, reviews, revenue, or testimonials. Never astroturf.
- No dark patterns, no real-money gambling mechanics (loot-box odds, cash-out, sweepstakes). Card/poker-style games use fictional currency only.
- Respect every platform's rules, including AI-content disclosure (Steam, Etsy) and anti-spam rules (itch.io).
- Original work only: no cloning names, art, or trade dress of existing games; no scraping or reselling others' content; no trademarked names in titles or tags.

## Owner-only actions (agents must stop and ask)
Creating accounts, accepting store agreements, entering payout/tax details, paying anything, publishing/listing, contacting third parties, and anything that is hard to reverse.

## Data
- Collect the minimum. No accounts or personal data in games at v1. Local saves only. No tracking beyond anonymous aggregate analytics we explicitly add and disclose.
- Do not target children under 13; no ads targeted at children.

## Quality
- A game is not "done" without: deterministic sim tests, balance bots, a browser smoke test, a screenshot review, and an owner playtest.
- Every bet has written acceptance criteria *before* building.
