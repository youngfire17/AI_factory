# The Tycoon Playbook (v1, 2026-10-10)

**Mindset rule:** an obstacle is a puzzle. For every one we find, the output is *how we overcome it or pivot*, never just a warning. Numbers stay honest ([verified]/[claimed]/[assumed]) because a tycoon player needs real prices and real ROI.

## The game
- **Loop:** cash engine → upgrades (tools, automation, helpers) → more engines → owned assets → bigger upgrades. **Reinvest 100%.** No owner draw until Rung R3.
- **Currencies:** Cash ($) · Owner hours (7–14/wk) · Proof (case studies, data, testimonials) · Assets (client book/MRR, templates, compliance pack, inventory, domains).
- **Core metric:** net $ per owner-hour, and % of work done by AI. Money buys automation; automation frees hours; freed hours run the next engine.

## Level 1 — the lemonade stand (days 0–90): Missed-Call Recovery for local trades
**Why this engine:** it is the only route in our model with positive expected value, it makes recurring revenue (a client book is an asset), and the buyer has urgent, expensive pain: one recovered HVAC repair call is worth ~$200–900; a replacement $11.6–14.1k; plumbing ~$300–500 per call; electrical ~$2.5k per job (all [claimed]). One recovered job pays for months of service.

| Obstacle found | How we beat it |
|---|---|
| The feature is commoditized ($29–$599/mo tools; Jobber $99 add-on; GoHighLevel $97–194) | **Sell the outcome and the service, not the tool.** Done-for-you setup + a weekly "jobs recovered" report for non-technical owners; one trade, one metro |
| No proof that owners will pay for done-for-you | **Free pilot + guarantee** ("recover ≥ 1 job in 30 days or you pay nothing"); optional hybrid pricing later ($99 base + per-booked-job fee; hybrid structures exist [claimed]). Pilots *are* the demand test |
| No audience | **Free Missed-Call Audit as the hook:** we call each prospect's business line at 3 times (e.g. weekday noon, evening, Saturday AM), log answered/voicemail/callback time, and send a one-page report with lost-revenue estimate using their own average ticket. Mystery-shop format is an established product [claimed]. Owner does the calls to business lines (allowed; no autodialer); AI builds lists and reports; a VA takes over later |
| Where to find prospects | State licensing open data (e.g. California CSLB monthly file, ~285k contractors [claimed]) + public listings; AI ranks by trade/city/review gaps. **Referral partners:** HVAC/plumbing supply houses, accountants, insurance agents, chambers, trade-association newsletters [claimed tactics] |
| Platform cost ($297/mo GoHighLevel) | **Stage the stack.** Pay-as-you-go first (SMS API + workflow + LLM); GoHighLevel only when ≥ 3 paying clients cover it (3 × $149 ≈ $447) |
| Texting legal risk (TCPA $500–$1,500 per message; 10DLC) | **Turn it into an asset:** a lawyer-reviewed *Compliance Pack* (informational-only reply, opt-out handling, FTC-safe review requests asked of ALL customers). Reusable for every client; sellable to other agencies later. 10DLC: sole-prop ≈ $4.50 + $15 + ~$2/mo (only for businesses without an EIN); standard brand ≈ $46 [claimed]; verify approval times |
| Owner's hours are capped | **Hire workers (the upgrade shop):** a Philippines VA at ~$6–12/h [claimed] for audit calls, list building, follow-ups once MRR supports it |

**Targets (all [assumed]; adjust after the first data):** day 14: 20 audits done, ≥ 5 real conversations · day 30: 3 pilots live · day 60: 2–3 paying clients (~$300–450 MRR) · day 90: 5–6 clients (~$750–$1,100 MRR at $149; net ~$500–800).
**Kill/pivot (day 30):** 20 audits + 20 conversations and no pilot → change offer (GBP/reviews, AI-answering add-on), change vertical, or change lane.

## Pivot tree (if a branch is blocked)
- Pilots don't convert → performance/revenue-share pricing, or sell the audit itself as a paid $49–99 report.
- Texting blocked or legally risky → email/voicemail callback + AI-answering via a partner platform.
- Trades unresponsive → same audit for another phone-driven, high-ticket niche (dental, med spa, law, towing, garage door, water-damage restoration).
- Price pressure → resell existing platforms with a setup fee and keep the service layer.
- First clients slow → lead with referral partners (supply houses/accountants/insurance agents) and borrow their audience.

## Weekly cadence (7–14 h)
~3–4 h audit calls and outreach follow-ups · ~2 h pilot/client ops · ~1 h side quests · ~1 h weekly review with the Co-CEO. AI drafts every list, report, message, bookkeeping entry, and weekly client report.
