# Heuristic design rubric (design-critic)

Score each 1–5 with one sentence of evidence from the build. **A high score does not prove the game is fun; only human playtests do.**

1. **Clarity** — within 30s a new player knows the goal and the next action.
2. **Agency** — decisions matter; at least 3 viable strategies; no always-best choice.
3. **Pacing** — first reward < 20s; a new decision every 30–60s; no dead stretches > 90s.
4. **Feedback** — every action has visible/audible response; numbers are readable.
5. **Progression** — the next goal is visible; the wall feels beatable with a different choice, not just waiting.
6. **Novelty** — one memorable mechanic or hook vs the closest incumbent.
7. **Polish** — consistent art/audio, no layout breaks at 360px, no jank.

Pass bar for S3 (vertical slice): ≥ 3.5 on every axis.
