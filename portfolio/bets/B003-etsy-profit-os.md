# B003 — Etsy Seller Profit OS

**Hook:** "Upload your Etsy sales CSV and see what you actually make": fees, ad costs, shipping, COGS, margin, profit per listing, break-even and minimum price, effective hourly wage.
**Buyer:** small Etsy sellers. **Price test:** $9–$19 one-time HTML tool; $7+/month later.
**Channel:** Etsy listing for the tool (sellers shop there), then own landing page.
**Why it scores well:** no backend needed for v1 (client-side HTML/JS), fully testable with fixture CSVs, no inventory, clear upgrade path to SaaS.

## Evidence status — [claimed]
Competing products at $8.55–$29.99 with dozens of reviews (from the source chat; unverified).

## Open questions
Exact current Etsy CSV export formats and fee schedule (verify against Etsy's own docs); whether sellers will pay versus use free spreadsheets; privacy stance for uploaded financial data (client-side only is a selling point).

## Acceptance criteria (draft, G2)
Parses real-format fixture CSVs; fee math matches Etsy's published fees to the cent on test cases; works offline; no data leaves the browser.

## Research update (2026-10-10)
Score 80 → 74. Free/cheap competition is heavy: Craftybase free spreadsheets, ZIK free fee calculator, Etsy templates ~$3–$10, ProfitTree free/~$5.99 mo. **$9–$19 pricing looks too high.** Reviews say eRank/Marmalead do not do profit tracking (possible gap). Low ceiling: treat as a fast, cheap demand test, not a business.
