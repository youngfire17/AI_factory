# B009 — Accessibility checker + fix guide

**Hook:** paste a URL, get a plain-English WCAG issue list with exact fixes and a shareable report — **no "instant compliance" promises** (FTC action against accessiBe over exactly that).
**Why it's here:** deadline/enforcement-driven buyers: EU Accessibility Act applicable since 2025-06-28; US federal web-accessibility suits up 27% to 3,117 in 2025 [claimed, vendor-relayed].
**Risks:** crowded (free scanners, overlay vendors); a scanner finds only a fraction of WCAG issues; legal-claim exposure; B2B buyers may need outreach we don't do; AI Overviews may eat search traffic.
**Gate to G1:** verified competitor pricing, primary text of EAA scope for small firms (microenterprise exemption), proof the cheapest paid tier is under $50/mo, and a distribution path that is not cold outreach.
