# B002 — Custom escape-room kits (Etsy → own site)

**Hook:** describe the party/theme/ages/time; get a complete printable escape room (story, puzzles, clue cards, answer chain, host guide, final code) validated by a solver.
**Buyer:** parents, teachers, party hosts, small team-builders. **Price test:** $5–$15 per kit.
**Channel:** Etsy listings first (marketplace search), own site later.

## Evidence status — [claimed], must verify (labs/market-research/verification-queue.md)
- A shop with ~33.5k sales / 2.7k reviews; kits at $5–$17; 5,000+ competing listings. **All from the source chat, unverified.**
- Existing AI escape-room generators exist (competition and validation).

## Risks
Crowded niche; IP (no "Harry Potter"/branded themes: use generic "wizard school", "detective"); Etsy requires AI disclosure and that the seller is the creative force; print-quality PDFs and puzzle solvability are product-critical.

## Gate to G1
Verified demand and price points, ≥ 3 differentiation angles competitors lack (personalization, solver-verified solvability, multi-age), compliance pass on Etsy's AI/creativity rules.

## Acceptance criteria (draft, G2)
Solver proves every kit solvable with a unique final code; PDF renders correctly on Letter and A4; 3 sample kits pass owner playtest.
