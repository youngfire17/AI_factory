# B010 — Daily Seed (experiment X3)

**Hook:** one hand-checkable puzzle per day, same for everyone (deterministic seed), share your result as a spoiler-free string.
**Why:** daily-puzzle genre scored 21/30 (highest AI-buildability and sim-testability; solvers prove every puzzle is solvable and difficulty-graded; low art). Shareable results are a built-in distribution loop that needs no outreach.
**Risks:** Steam puzzle medians ≈ $1,000 or less [claimed]; mobile puzzle pool is incumbent/UA-owned; word-list licensing/origin must be checked; novelty versus Wordle-likes; ad revenue per play unknown.
**Metric:** D2/D7 return, share clicks, any tip/supporter signal. **Kill:** D7 < 5% after 30 days.
**Greenlight needs:** teardown of the 5 closest daily games, monetization memo, clearance memo (names/IP/word lists).
